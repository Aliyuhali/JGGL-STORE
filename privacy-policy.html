JGGL-STORE Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect customer information.

Information We Collect

JGGL-STORE may collect information such as your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, order details and account information when you register, place an order or update your profile.

How We Use Your Information

We use customer information to create and manage accounts, process orders, arrange deliveries, communicate order updates, provide customer support and improve our services.

Information Protection

JGGL-STORE takes reasonable steps to protect customer information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss or disclosure.

Information Sharing

Customer information will not be sold. Information may only be shared when necessary for order fulfilment, delivery, payment processing, legal compliance or service protection.

Customer Rights

Customers may review and update their profile information, delivery address and password. Account deletion options may also be provided, subject to legal and transaction record requirements.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when JGGL-STORE services, technologies or legal obligations change.
