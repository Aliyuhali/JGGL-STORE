Who We Are

JGGL-STORE is an online shopping platform operated by Janjunawan Galadi Global Limited. The platform is being developed to serve customers with retail, wholesale, bulk and daily-basis products through a trusted and convenient digital marketplace.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make quality products accessible to customers through a simple, secure and reliable shopping experience while supporting efficient order management, customer service and delivery operations.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a trusted and widely accessible digital marketplace that connects customers with essential products and supports the growth of modern commerce.

Our Core Values

Trust, honesty, customer satisfaction, product quality, reliability, innovation, accessibility and continuous improvement guide the development and operation of JGGL-STORE.

What We Offer

JGGL-STORE is designed to support food products, drinks, household items, electronics and other daily-use goods. Customers can browse products, manage carts and wishlists, place orders, track order status and manage their accounts.

Business Model

The platform supports retail, wholesale and bulk purchasing so that individuals, families, retailers and businesses can select the purchasing option that best suits their needs.

Our Commitment

We are committed to protecting customer information, providing accurate product details, improving service quality and developing a secure platform for customers, administrators and business operations.
